# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Everyone taking part in this project should:

- communicate respectfully and precisely;
- critique technical claims and decisions, not people;
- avoid harassment, threats, trolling, and discriminatory conduct;
- avoid publishing another person's private information;
- respect the documented Practice Relay and MvEI product boundaries;
- avoid pressuring others to accept unsupported certification, adoption, or
  pilot claims.

This applies to repository discussions, review, issue triage, and other project
spaces the maintainers control.

## Enforcement

Sensitive conduct reports go through the confidential route documented in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). That route is not configured in this candidate and
must be tested before publication. Do not put private details in a public issue.

Once the route and maintainers are confirmed, maintainers may warn participants,
limit participation, remove content, or ban participants. Enforcement decisions
should record the relevant behavior and protect private information.
