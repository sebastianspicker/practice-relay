# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and precisely;
- critique technical claims and decisions, not people;
- avoid harassment, threats, trolling, and discriminatory conduct;
- avoid publishing another person's private information;
- respect the documented Practice Relay and MvEI product boundaries;
- avoid pressure to accept unsupported certification, adoption, firstness, or pilot claims.

These expectations apply to repository discussions, review, issue triage, and other project spaces controlled by the maintainers.

## Enforcement

Sensitive conduct reports must use the confidential route documented in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). That route is not configured in this candidate and must be tested before publication. Do not put private details in a public issue.

Once the route and maintainers are confirmed, maintainers may warn participants, limit participation, remove content, or ban participants in response to violations. Enforcement decisions should record the relevant behavior and protect private information.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant but is not a verbatim copy.
